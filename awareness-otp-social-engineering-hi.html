OTP और सोशल इंजीनियरिंग

हमलावर लोगों को OTP और निजी जानकारी बताने के लिए बहकाते हैं।

पूरा हिन्दी लेख जल्द जोड़ा जाएगा। नीचे की सूची अंग्रेज़ी में है।

Warning signs

  • Caller claims to be bank or KYC staff
  • Asks you to read out an OTP
  • Creates fear or urgency

Prevention tips

  • Never share OTPs with anyone
  • Hang up and call the official number
  • Banks never ask for OTP

If an incident occurs

  1. Stop communicating with the scammer and do not send more money.
  2. If money was lost, call 1930 immediately (financial cyber fraud helpline) and contact your bank.
  3. Report at the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
  4. Save screenshots, transaction IDs and messages as evidence.
  5. Change passwords of affected accounts and enable two-step verification.

Official reporting

  • National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal
  • Helpline: 1930 (financial cyber fraud)
